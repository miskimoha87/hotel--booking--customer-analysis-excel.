# 🔎 6. Findings & Recommendations

### Screenshot: `Fidings and Recommendations.png`

![Findings and Recommendations](screenshoots/Fidings%20and%20Recommendations.png)

The final stage of the project is to convert the analysis into actionable business insights.

## 🔍 Key Findings

### 1. Booking Records

A total of **292 booking records** were analyzed.

### 2. Booking Status

Completed bookings were the largest category with **158 bookings**.

Other booking statuses included:

* Confirmed: 58
* Cancelled: 55
* No-Show: 21

### 3. Room Type

**Standard rooms** were the most booked room type with **144 bookings**.

This indicates strong customer demand for Standard rooms.

### 4. Customer Type

The customer distribution was:

* New Customers: 135
* Returning Customers: 100
* Corporate Customers: 57

New customers represent the largest customer group.

### 5. Length of Stay

Returning customers had the longest average stay, at approximately **6.20 nights**.

This indicates that retaining customers may contribute to longer stays and potentially higher revenue.

---

# 💡 Recommendations

Based on the findings, the following recommendations are suggested:

### 1. Promote Standard Rooms

Since Standard rooms have the highest booking volume, the hotel should ensure sufficient availability and consider promoting this room category.

### 2. Improve Customer Retention

New customers represent the largest customer group. Loyalty programs, special offers, and return-customer discounts could encourage them to book again.

### 3. Develop Corporate Packages

Corporate customers could be targeted with special packages, long-stay offers, and business travel discounts.

### 4. Reduce Cancellations and No-Shows

The hotel can reduce cancellations and no-shows by using:

* Booking reminders
* Confirmation messages
* Flexible cancellation policies
* Advance payment options

### 5. Monitor Revenue

Management should regularly monitor completed booking revenue and room performance through the dashboard.
